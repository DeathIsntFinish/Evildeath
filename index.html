About Diwali

🪔 The Festival of Lights

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is one of the most celebrated festivals in India. It is known as the Festival of Lights.


People decorate their homes with diyas, candles, rangoli and beautiful lights. Families come together, exchange sweets and gifts, and celebrate happiness and togetherness.

Why DO We Celebrate Diwali?

Diwali has great cultural and religious importance. In the Ramayana tradition, it is associated with the return of Lord Rama, Sita and Lakshmana to Ayodhya after a long period away from their kingdon.


People lights diyas to symbolize light overcooming darkness and good overcoming evil. Diwali is also celebrated in different ways across India and is associated with prosperity, new beginnings and family gatherings.
